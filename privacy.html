DeskBoard privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

DeskBoard is a private, personal dashboard app used only by the person who built it. This page explains what the app does with data from a connected Google account.

What DeskBoard accesses

When its owner signs in with Google, DeskBoard requests access to Google Drive. It uses that access only to read the data files in the owner's own "DeskBoard" folder and to add small files to that folder when an item is ticked off in the app.

How the data is used

The data is shown on the owner's own devices and is used for nothing else. DeskBoard does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not use it to train AI models.

Storage and sharing

DeskBoard has no server of its own. The Google sign-in is stored encrypted on the device where it was entered, and data read from Google Drive stays on that device. DeskBoard does not transfer or share Google user data with any third party.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time by disconnecting Google Drive in the app's settings or from the Google Account page under "Third-party apps and services". Uninstalling the app deletes the data it stored on the device.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the issue tracker of the repository that hosts this page.
